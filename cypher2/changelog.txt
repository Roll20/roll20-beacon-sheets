Cypher System 2 Character Sheet - Changelog
============================================

2026-09-30
- The sheet now reads character files that use the Old Gods of Appalachia and
  The Magnus Archives rules. Import one from Cypher Tools the same way you
  always have.
- A game that uses the damage track gets it in place of the wound boxes. The
  Damage & Recovery panel lists Hale, Impaired, Debilitated and Dead. Click the
  marker beside a step to say that is where your character is, and it fills in.
  Click the step's name instead to read what that step does to the character. A
  line under the track says how a character moves up and down it. The sheet never
  moves the step for you.
- To turn the track on or off, click the pencil on the Damage & Recovery panel
  and use the Damage track checkbox. Turning it off brings the wound boxes back
  with the values they had. It also forgets which step you were on, so turning
  the track back on starts again at Hale.
- Switching the Damage track or Stress checkbox off throws away what that rule
  was holding: the step your character was on, or your Stress points and
  supernatural levels. There is no way to get those back from the sheet, so the
  checkbox now asks twice. The first click turns the label red and tells you what
  it is about to clear, and only a second click does it. Use the other checkbox
  instead, or close the pencil, and nothing is cleared. It asks only when there is
  something to lose. A track still sitting at Hale, or Stress at zero, switches
  off on the first click, the way the bullet above describes.
- In a game that uses the damage track, a guided roll that spends Effort and
  empties a Pool now says so on the chat card, because emptying a Pool moves a
  character one step down the track. A game with the track off is unchanged and
  gets no such line.

2026-09-17
- You can drag a page out of the Cypher compendium and drop it onto the sheet.
  An ability, a skill, a cypher, an artifact or a piece of equipment lands as a
  new row in the right list, the sheet switches to the tab holding that list, and
  the new row is highlighted so you can see where it went. Drop the same page
  twice and you get two rows, because the sheet does not assume a duplicate is a
  mistake.
- A page the sheet cannot turn into a row says so instead of doing nothing. A
  focus or a type page tells you to pick it in the character's sentence. A page
  with nothing for the sheet to read says the page is malformed. A page written
  for a newer sheet says the sheet needs updating, and one written for an older
  sheet says it cannot be read.
- An NPC stat block refuses a drop, because its rows are not the character
  lists.
- A viewer who can see a character but cannot change it should not drag onto it.
  The row appears for a moment and then disappears, because the server refuses
  the change. Nothing is damaged and nothing is saved. This is a known rough
  edge and it is being fixed.

2026-09-16
- A character renamed in the Roll20 journal keeps its new name. Before, the
  next edit on the sheet could put the old name back. Portrait, bio and GM
  notes changes stick the same way.
- Importing the wrong file now says what it is. If you picked the Foundry
  export in Cypher Tools, the sheet tells you to export again with the Roll20
  option. Problems in a file are listed by field, and a refused export says
  where on the sheet to fix it.

2026-09-15
- Attacks can now be rolled. Click the d20 beside an attack to open the guided
  roller for it. Your training with the attack, a light weapon, and the
  attack's modifier ease the roll automatically, and each one is listed so you
  can see where the ease came from.
- Enter the creature's level instead of a difficulty. The chat card says Hit or
  Miss, and shows your damage only on a hit.
- You can spend Effort on damage as well as on hitting. Each level adds 3
  damage. Both kinds of Effort share one cost, so your Edge comes off once for
  the attack.
- A natural 17 or 18 adds its bonus damage to the total. On a 19 or 20 the card
  offers a choice between the special effect and extra damage. A natural 20
  refunds all the Effort you spent.

2026-09-14
- New characters now open the Settings window automatically the first time the
  sheet is opened, so you can pick a skin and your roll options right away.
  It only happens once per character. Characters you already have are not
  affected.
- GMs can turn any sheet into an NPC stat block. Open the gear menu and set
  Sheet type to NPC. The card follows the Cypher GM's Guide, with level,
  health, damage, armor, movement, combat actions, GM intrusions and more.
  Switching back to Character keeps everything the sheet had.
- An NPC's health can be linked to a token bar.
- Only GMs see an NPC's stat block. Players see a short notice instead, but
  keep NPC journal entries hidden from players, since the sheet only hides the
  view.
- GMs can export a creature to a file and import it onto another NPC sheet.
